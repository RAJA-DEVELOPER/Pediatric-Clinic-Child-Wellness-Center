Our History

Our Journey

From a single clinic to a regional healthcare leader — a story written one family at a time.

2009

The Beginning

Founded by Dr. Robert Kim with a single clinic and a vision to bring world-class pediatric care to every family in the region.

2011

Vaccination Expansion

Expanded our vaccination program to serve 500+ families, partnering with the National Immunization Program for comprehensive immunization services.

2013

Second Location

Opened our second clinic location and added a dedicated Developmental Pediatrics department to support children with special developmental needs.

2016

Digital Innovation

Launched our digital parent portal and telemedicine services, making quality pediatric care accessible from anywhere at any time.

2019

Regional Recognition

Received the prestigious "Best Pediatric Clinic" regional award, recognizing our commitment to clinical excellence and patient-centered care.

2022

Third Location & 5000 Families

Opened our third clinic location at Northgate Center and celebrated a milestone of 5,000+ families trusting PediaCare with their children's health.

2024

AI-Powered Care

Launched our AI-powered growth tracking system, providing parents with real-time insights into their child's developmental progress and health trends.

Frequently Asked Questions

PediaCare serves patients from birth (newborns) through 18 years of age. Our specialized neonatology team handles premature and high-risk newborns, while our adolescent health specialists provide age-appropriate care for teens. We believe in long-term relationships with families, so we strive to be your child's healthcare home from their first breath through young adulthood.

Yes! PediaCare accepts most major insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, Humana, Medicare, and many more. We also offer flexible self-pay pricing and payment plans for uninsured families. Please call our reception or use our online insurance verification tool to confirm your specific plan is accepted before your visit.

Appointments can be booked through our online patient portal, by calling any of our three clinic locations, or by using the "Book Appointment" button at the top of our website. For urgent same-day needs, please call directly — we reserve slots for sick children who need to be seen quickly. Telemedicine appointments can also be booked directly through our portal.

For your child's first visit, please bring your insurance card and a valid photo ID, your child's previous medical records and vaccination history, any current medications (or a written list with dosages), and your completed new patient registration forms (available online). Arriving 15 minutes early helps us get everything set up so the doctor can spend maximum time with your child.

Absolutely! PediaCare offers secure, HIPAA-compliant telemedicine consultations for a wide range of non-emergency concerns including follow-up visits, medication reviews, behavioral questions, and minor illness guidance. Telehealth appointments are available 7 days a week, including evenings. Book through our patient portal, download our app, or call us to schedule your virtual visit from the comfort of home.
